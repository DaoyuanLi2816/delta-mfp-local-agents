# Task Generation Notes

The local task suite is generated by `code/benchmarks/local_task_suite.py`.

The submitted suite contains 120 deterministic tasks:

- 4 families: calendar, refund/order, file/email, inventory.
- 3 difficulty levels: easy, medium, hard.
- 10 tasks per family and difficulty.

Each task includes:

- initial simulator state;
- available tool schemas;
- required effects;
- forbidden effects and state invariants;
- correct and faulty step templates;
- family, difficulty, required-step count, and fault-compatible metadata.

Difficulty is operational:

- easy: 2-3 explicit tool steps with all fields supplied;
- medium: 4-6 steps with one ambiguity or state dependency;
- hard: 6-10 steps with memory/state dependency, policy constraint, or boundary-like content.

The suite is controlled by design. Its purpose is to isolate tool-interface, replay, and repair mechanisms under local inference rather than to claim broad benchmark coverage.
